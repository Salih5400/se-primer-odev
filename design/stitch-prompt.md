Create exactly THREE mobile app screens for a Turkish software engineering learning game called “SE Primer”: 1) onboarding / entry, 2) one reusable incident decision screen, 3) the ending screen. These are phone screens, 390 × 844 px, arranged side by side on one canvas. Do not create a landing page, desktop layout, dashboard, extra screens, or decorative cover.

PRODUCT
The player is the on-call software engineer for a fictional healthcare appointment system. At 08:00, after last night's release, the system is down and 300 patients are waiting. The player makes three decisions about architecture, testing, and KVKK. The experience is educational; use no real patient data.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Design a calm, trustworthy, editorial mobile interface. Light warm-neutral background (#F7F8F5), white content areas, deep navy text (#18242E), muted gray-green secondary text, deep teal primary actions (#146B5A), and a restrained coral warning (#A33F35) only for the data exposure ending. Use system sans-serif / Google Sans, 24 px screen gutters, generous spacing, thin borders, 12–16 px corner radii, and subtle or no shadows. Use no gradients, no glassmorphism, no medical stock photos, no hospital cross clichés, and no dashboard-style card grid. Keep contrast high, type comfortable, controls at least 44 px tall, and never use color alone to communicate status.

SCREEN 1 — GİRİŞ
Show brand “SE PRIMER”, heading “Vardiyaya hazır mısın?”, and subtitle “Bir yazılım mühendisi olarak karar ver. Sonuçlarını gör.” Include three compact, clearly labeled questions:
- “Deneyim seviyen”: “Yeni başlıyorum” / “Deneyimliyim”
- “Rolün”: “Geliştirici” / “Test mühendisi” / “Ürün ekibi”
- “Bugün ne öğrenmek istiyorsun?”: “Mimari” / “Test” / “Veri güvenliği”
Show one selected value for each, and a prominent button “Nöbeti başlat”. Keep the screen airy and scannable, not like a settings form.

SCREEN 2 — SAHNE / KARAR 1
At the top show “SE PRIMER” and progress “Karar 1 / 3”. Show the incident heading “08:00 · Randevu sistemi yanıt vermiyor” and context “Dün gece yeni sürüm çıktı. 300 hasta bekliyor.” Then label “KARAR 1 · MİMARİ” and ask “Önce ne yaparsın?” Present exactly two accessible answer buttons:
A. “Eski sürüme geri dön” with supporting text “Önceki sürümü yeniden devreye al.”
B. “Canlıda yama yap” with supporting text “Mevcut sürüm üzerinde düzeltme yayınla.”
Include small actions “Bir ipucu al” and “Tutor’a sor”. This is one reusable scene layout; add a small annotation outside the phone screen with the other decision states, not as extra screens: Decision 2 · Test — “Önce kısa bir smoke test çalıştır” / “Test etmeden hemen yayına al”; Decision 3 · KVKK — “Logdaki adı maskele” / “Ham logu ekiple paylaş”. In experienced mode a subtle 60-second timer appears; in beginner mode the optional hint action appears.

SCREEN 3 — SON / SAFE SAMPLE
Show safe outcome as the visual sample. Heading “08:25 · Sistem ayakta”. Summary “Logu maskelediğin için kişisel veri korundu.” Add a compact three-row “Kararların” recap and a clear button “Yeniden başlat”. Outside the phone screen, annotate an alternate variant on the SAME layout (do not make a fourth screen): heading “Sistem ayakta, veri açığa çıktı”; summary “Ham logu paylaşman kişisel veriyi açığa çıkardı. Bu bir KVKK ihlalidir.” The alternate state uses a restrained coral warning icon and explicit text while preserving accessible contrast.

Use the Turkish copy exactly. Prioritize clear hierarchy, touch-friendly spacing, and realistic phone composition. Show only three phone screens total; annotations about alternate states can sit outside the phone frames.
